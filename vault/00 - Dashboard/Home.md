## 🧭 How to use this vault

1. **Start at [[YC Countdown]]** every morning — it is the single source of truth for what must be true by Nov 2, 2026.
2. **One note, one idea.** Notes link by `[[wikilink]]`; follow the graph instead of searching. If a fact appears in two notes, one of them is wrong — fix it at the source ([[Key Figures]] owns all numbers).
3. **Log every customer conversation** with the `Discovery Call` template in `98 - Templates`; the [[YC Application]] traction claim (25+ conversations) is built from those files.
4. **Record irreversible choices** (pricing, architecture, equity) with the `Decision Log` template.
5. **Build results are not edited by hand** — [[Build Status]] is regenerated by CI; link to it, never write it.
6. Weekly review: update `status:` in frontmatter (`draft → active → done`), then walk [[8-Week Sprint Plan]] exit criteria against reality.

> [!warning] Blueprint, not business
> *"The single most common cause of accelerator rejection is an idea chosen from a report like this one and never confronted with a real customer conversation."* — The next 25 phone calls matter more than the next 25 pages of planning.
