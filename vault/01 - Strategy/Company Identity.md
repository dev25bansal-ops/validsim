## The one-sentence thesis

> [!important]
> There is **no productized CI/CD layer between "model checkpoint" and "deploy to fleet."** We build that missing layer.

Robot foundation model companies — Physical Intelligence (**$5.6B** valuation), Skild AI (**$1.4B** Series C at **$14–15B** valuation), FieldAI, and dozens more — now ship policy updates as frequently as software teams ship code. But unlike software, a bad robot deploy can drop a **$50K** component, damage a production line, or injure a human coworker. Every lab today validates with hand-built test scenes, small teleoperation trials, and judgment calls ([[Problem Statement]]).

## The analogy that sells itself

> *"In 2010, software teams deployed code with FTP uploads and prayer. GitHub Actions, CircleCI, and Jenkins turned that into automated pipelines. Today, no serious software team ships without CI/CD.*
>
> *In 2026, robot teams deploy model updates with hand-built test scenes and judgment calls. We are building the CI/CD layer for robots — before the first major deployment failure makes it mandatory."*

The CI/CD market for software was **$0 in 2010** and is now a **$10B+** category. The robot CI/CD market is at **$0 in 2026**. We are building at the inflection point ([[Market Sizing (TAM SAM SOM)]]).
