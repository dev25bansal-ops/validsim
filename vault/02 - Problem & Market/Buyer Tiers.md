# 🎯 Buyer Tiers

Who feels the pain ([[Pain Quantified]]), when they buy, and what they pay. Ordering is the GTM sequence ([[Go-to-Market]]).

## Tier 1 — Robot Foundation Model Companies *(Immediate Buyers, 2026)*

| Company | What they ship |
|---|---|
| **Physical Intelligence** | pi0 model updates — **$5.6B valuation** |
| **Skild AI** | General-purpose robot FM — **$1.4B Series C at $14–15B valuation** |
| **FieldAI** | Autonomous robot intelligence |
| **1X Technologies** | NEO humanoid |
| **Figure AI** | Figure 02 humanoid |
| **Apptronik** | Apollo humanoid |
| **Agility Robotics** | Digit humanoid |
| **Boston Dynamics** | Spot, Atlas (Hyundai) |

- **Total: ~50–100 companies globally in 2026** → 150–200 by 2028, 300–500 by 2030
- Avg. annual spend capacity: **$50K–$500K**
- Why first: they ship weekly/bi-weekly ([[Why Now (2026)]], Force 2) and feel regression pain per-update.
- Design-partner offer: free validation runs (up to 50/month) for feedback + LOI ([[Go-to-Market]] Phase 1).

## Tier 2 — Humanoid OEMs & Integrators *(Year 1–2 Buyers)*

- **Tesla Optimus, Unitree, UBTECH, Sanctuary AI**
- **Total: ~200–500 companies by 2028** (OEMs 200–500 by 2030; integrators 2,000–5,000 by 2030)
- Avg. annual spend: **$100K–$1M** (OEMs), **$10K–$100K** (integrators)
- Buying trigger: enterprise customers demanding safety evidence before pilots ([[Pain Quantified]]).

## Tier 3 — Fleet Operators & Enterprise Deployers *(Year 2–3 Buyers)*

| Vertical | Named prospects |
|---|---|
| 3PL logistics | Amazon Robotics, GXO, DHL |
| Automotive | BMW, Mercedes, Toyota |
| Electronics | Foxconn, Samsung |

- **Total: ~1,000+ fleet operators by 2029** (50–100 in 2026 → 300–500 in 2028 → 1,000–2,000 in 2030)
- Avg. annual spend: **$20K–$200K**
- Buying trigger: fleet-scale validation — "testing on 1 robot ≠ testing on 1,000" ([[Data Flow]] gate).

## Tier 4 — Insurers, Regulators & Compliance Bodies *(Year 3+)*

- **Insurers:** Munich Re, Swiss Re, Lloyd's of London
- **Certification:** UL, TÜV, BSI
- **Regulators:** OSHA, EU Machinery Regulation
- **Total: mandatory spend on every deployment** — count 10–20 (2026) → 100–200 (2030), **$50K–$500K/yr**
- Strategy: partner, don't sell ([[Compliance]]); once insurers adopt our scorecard format it is the de-facto standard ([[Moat]]).
