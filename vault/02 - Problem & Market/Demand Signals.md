# 📈 Demand Signals

Observable market evidence that the [[Problem Statement]] is being paid for *right now* — not a future thesis. Use these five signals whenever an investor says "but does anyone care?"

## The five signals

| # | Signal | Value | Why it converts to demand for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Robotics VC funding (2025) | **$27.6B** | Creates an entire supplier ecosystem needing tooling; every dollar raised buys infrastructure ([[Market Sizing (TAM SAM SOM)]]) |
| 2 | Robot FM company funding (2026) | **$3.92B across 9 deals** | Every one of those companies needs validation before deployment — they are our Tier-1 ICP ([[Buyer Tiers]]) |
| 3 | Humanoid unit volume | **10,000+ units/year by 2027**, 38,000/year by 2030 | Every deployment needs safety evidence; volume multiplies per-deploy validation cost |
| 4 | US manufacturing labor gap | **449,000 jobs unfilled** (March 2025) → **2.1M projected unfilled by 2030** | Accelerates robot adoption → accelerates need for validation |
| 5 | China state policy | **2026–2030 five-year plan** lists humanoid robots as strategic priority | Global demand acceleration; US labs must ship faster to compete → less time for manual validation |

## Supporting macro data points

- **$18.8B** robotics funding in H1 2026 alone (Value Add VC) — pace exceeds 2025's full-year figure
- **$8.7B** into humanoid robotics 2026 YTD (Dealroom) — ~2× all of 2025
- **$1T/year** economic drag from skilled-trades shortage (JLL, April 2026) — the reason automation spend is structural, not cyclical
- AI data-center power constraints (Gartner/Spheron, June 2026) — GPU-parallel simulation efficiency is itself a differentiator ([[Tech Stack]])

> [!note] Signal vs. noise discipline
> VC funding is *customer-creation* evidence, not revenue evidence. The demand proof we actually own is behavioral: **weekly/bi-weekly model update cadence** at Physical Intelligence, Skild AI, and FieldAI ([[Why Now (2026)]], Force 2) — each update is a validation event with no current owner.

## How we add signals on the ground

Each discovery call is instrumented to test one signal ([[Pain Quantified]] anchors):

- [ ] Does the lab ship policy updates ≥ bi-weekly? (Force 2)
- [ ] Do they have a live humanoid/AMR pilot with an enterprise customer? (Force 3)
- [ ] Has an insurer or customer asked for deployment evidence? (Force 5)
- [ ] What do they spend on bespoke validation tooling today? ($200K–$500K anchor)

Results feed the [[YC Countdown]] traction checklist and [[KPIs]] (50 discovery conversations by month 6 → 100 by month 12).

## Falsification — what would kill the thesis

- Funding winter cuts FM lab headcount below validation budgets (Risk: [[Risk Register]] #6 compute/cost sensitivity)
- Humanoid deployments slip 1–2 years → Tier 2/3 buying delayed ([[Risk Register]] #3; mitigation: robot-type-agnostic validation for AMRs/arms/drones)
- NVIDIA ships a productized CI layer on top of Isaac → [[Risk Register]] #2, [[Competitive Landscape]]
